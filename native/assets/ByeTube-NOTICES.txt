ByeTube Windows adapter in MCRF
Engine: ByeDPI v0.17.3, hufrea, MIT license (LICENSE.byedpi).
https://github.com/hufrea/byedpi
Strategy reference: StressOzz/Zapret-Manager, ByeTube section, retrieved 2026-10-04.
https://github.com/StressOzz/Zapret-Manager
The original OpenWrt service is not executed on Windows. MCRF uses its own sing-box TUN and a localhost-only ByeDPI SOCKS service. Linux-only -S strategies are excluded, not silently altered. UDP/QUIC for selected ByeTube routes is blocked so browsers may retry over TCP.
